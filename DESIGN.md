# Manonton

Reading this as: a watch-party application for friends, in the visual language of an independent cinema programme. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

The user's direction is a film-watching site with a polished catalogue, now named Manonton, with both light and dark themes. Film and room controls take precedence over marketing sections. Existing destinations are retained.

- Identity: the word Manonton in text, without an invented symbol or portrait. Short Indonesian labels and film titles carry the character.
- Palette: warm paper and charcoal-green neutrals, with a rust-red action colour. Green and amber are reserved for actual connection/readiness states. The player stays black in both themes to preserve the viewing environment.
- Type: Outfit, already self-hosted by the app, has rounded letterforms suitable for an informal film club. Body text remains regular weight and comfortably sized. Film titles use a larger, tighter register; controls do not use decorative uppercase tracking.
- Composition: the landing page introduces watching together and offers two real routes. The dashboard prioritizes the user's rooms and a join-code form. The library is a catalogue of actual media titles and processing states. Account pages are compact forms. Watching is player-first, with chat beside it when space permits and people below it.
- Admin: distinct tabs separate users, actual audit records and system jobs. Editing is a native modal drawer on the right, preserving table context and trapping focus. A phone uses a full-width drawer.
- Surfaces: 6px controls, 10px content surfaces, full circles only for the hold-to-talk control and initial avatars. Borders define controls; shadows only separate the drawer from the page.
- Spacing: compact controls within a task, more space between tasks. Phone, intermediate and wide layouts are designed separately; navigation becomes a labelled menu on narrow screens.
- Motion: hover/pressed feedback, a short drawer transition only when motion is allowed, and functional reaction feedback. No decorative entrances, perpetual glow or background animation.
- Artwork: no invented movie posters, people, testimonials or metrics. Actual data appears when available; title-led media rows are preferable to fabricated product imagery.
- Light mode: semantic CSS tokens apply to every page, dialog, table, field, toast and status. The selected theme persists locally and the initial render follows the system preference until a user chooses.
- Copy: name the action and result. Remove generic English marketing labels, repeated slogans, ornamental arrows and duplicate primary actions. Reactions remain emoji because selecting an emoji is the actual interaction.

No payment, paid tier, customer testimonial or usage claim is introduced. Account administration is a free-service foundation, not a claim that organization tenancy, email verification, recovery, billing or production operations are complete.
